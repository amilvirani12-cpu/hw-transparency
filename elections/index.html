This is an independent project maintained by Amil Virani, CPA — a district resident. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by the Hewlett-Woodmere Union Free School District or its Board of Education. Turnout figures are compiled from the district's public poll lists; this page publishes aggregate counts only. Certified ballot counts come from the district's official tally sheet for each vote (Proposition 1, the school budget). Prior years were reconstructed after the fact from poll lists pulled during election day, so their time-of-day windows follow whenever those lists were pulled. The current election refreshes automatically during election day.
